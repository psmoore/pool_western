Fit Lane Swim

25 m short course Saturday–Thursday · 50 m long course Fridays

Mornings 6:45–9:00 and middays 11:00–2:00 Monday to Friday; evenings 4:00–6:00 Monday and 4:00–7:00 Tuesday, Thursday and Friday; Saturday 1:00–4:00 and Sunday 9:30–12:30. No evening session on Wednesday.

Live from Pool Relay · open the full calendar →  —  Friday's three sessions are in the 50 m pool.

Long course, on the day it happens

A swimmer training for a 50 m meet wants to know one thing: when is the pool long course? The answer is a sentence above the table and an (LC) after each Friday time.

On the calendar Friday is the long-course day, and every session says so, week after week through December 23.

Questions for Campus Recreation Aquatics

Everything here was transcribed from the Schedules/Hours page. These are the places where it goes quiet or where we had to make a call — each one is also written on the calendar entry itself.

  • GapDoes Fit Lane Swim run during Reading Week?
    The building keeps adjusted hours October 13–16, and the pool section doesn't say whether its sessions change. The calendar shows the normal week.
  • OursWhich tank is the 2SLGBTQIA+ swim in?
    The other Saturday swims are marked (S). The 2SLGBTQIA+ swim, 12:00–1:00, has no tank code, so we put it in the shallow tank beside them.
  • GapHow many lanes in each tank?
    The schedule names tanks, not lanes. With lane counts, each Fit Lane block could show how many lanes are open — and Tuesday and Thursday's “S/D” sessions how many in each.
  • GapWhat happens in the December exam period?
    The building has exam hours; the pool section says only that its hours change for Western holidays. Say which sessions move and the calendar shows them on their dates.
